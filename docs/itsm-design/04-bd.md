## 5. Pricing Model

### SaaS Subscription — Per Agent Seat

| Tier | Price | Agents | End Users | Key AI Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $25/agent/mo | Up to 10 | Unlimited | Ticket classification, KB suggest |
| **Growth** | $55/agent/mo | Up to 50 | Unlimited | + Duplicate detection, reply suggestions, semantic KB search |
| **Pro** | $90/agent/mo | Unlimited | Unlimited | + Virtual agent, KB auto-draft, predictive maintenance, analytics |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | + SLA, SSO/SAML, custom AI model, dedicated support, SLA guarantee |

> End users (requesters) are never charged — only agents who work on tickets.

### Add-ons
| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|

| White-label (MSP) | +$200/mo flat |
| Data residency (EU/US) | +$150/mo flat |
| Advanced analytics dashboard | +$20/agent/mo |

### MSP / Reseller Model
- Volume discount: 20% off Growth/Pro for MSPs managing 10+ tenant accounts
- White-label: custom domain, custom branding, your own pricing to end clients
- Revenue share: 15% referral commission for partners

## 7. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Build & Seed (Months 1–6)
- **Launch on 99technologies.com** — position as a flagship product of the 99T platform
- **Beta with 5–10 design partners** (existing Project-IQ customers or contacts)
  - Offer free Pro tier for 6 months in exchange for feedback + testimonials
- **Product Hunt launch** at end of Phase 1
- **Content SEO**: "ServiceNow alternatives", "ITSM for startups", "AI helpdesk comparison"

### Phase 2 — Traction (Months 6–18)
- **G2 / Capterra presence**: solicit reviews from beta users
- **MSP channel program**: recruit 10 MSPs as resellers
- **Integration marketplace**: Slack, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, Jira (webhook-based, Phase 2 of product)
- **LinkedIn outreach**: target IT managers at 200–2,000 employee companies
- **Founder-led sales**: direct outreach to warm leads, demo-to-trial funnel

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 18–36)
- **Inbound-led**: SEO + G2 ranking driving 60%+ of signups
- **Inside sales team**: 2 AEs for Growth/Pro deals > $10K ARR
- **Partner ecosystem**: IT consultancies reselling IQ-ITSM to their clients
- **Enterprise motion**: dedicated AE for deals > $50K ARR

## 8. Competitive Differentiation

| Capability | IQ-ITSM | ServiceNow | Freshservice | Jira SM |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| AI ticket classification | ✓ native | Add-on $ | Limited | No |
| Semantic KB search | ✓ native | Add-on $ | No | No |
| AI reply suggestions | ✓ native | No | Limited | No |
| Auto KB drafting from tickets | ✓ native | No | No | No |
| Predictive asset maintenance | ✓ native | Add-on $ | No | No |
| Virtual agent (RAG chatbot) | ✓ native | Separate product $ | Limited | No |
| Multi-tenant SaaS (MSP-ready) | ✓ | Custom | Limited | No |
| IAM integration (99T) | ✓ native | — | — | — |
| Time to first value | < 1 day | 6–12 months | 1–2 weeks | 1–3 days |
| Entry price | $25/agent | ~$150K+/yr | $19/agent | $17/agent |

**Moat**: AI is not a bolt-on — it's woven into the data model (embeddings, predictions, classifications stored per ticket/asset/article). Competitors would need to re-architect, not just add a feature.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI cost exceeds revenue at scale | Medium | High | Per-tenant token budgets; tiered AI features by plan |
| ServiceNow launches aggressive mid-market offering | Low | High | Speed to market; MSP channel lock-in; our IAM integration moat |
| Commoditisation (OpenAI wraps ITSM) | Medium | Medium | Data network effects — our embeddings/classifications improve per tenant over time |
| Customer data concerns with LLM | Medium | Medium | PII masking; opt-out flag; on-premise LLM option roadmap (Ollama) |
| Slow enterprise sales cycle | High | Medium | Self-serve trial → land with Growth → expand to Pro; avoid pure enterprise-only motion early |
